chaztils v2.4.0 brings new modules, more customisation, and major performance improvements to your gameplay!

New Modules — Introducing Nick Hider and Bingo.

Custom Tab List Improvements — Player skins and heads now render in the tab list, with a new line separator option and a more compact layout.

Bedwars Overlay Upgrades — Blacklist tags now display even with an invalid Hypixel API key, a new timer tracks when your key expires, and caching reduces API calls.

More Chat Customisation — Edit “FINAL KILL!” and “BED DESTRUCTION >” messages, compress shop purchase messages with new formatting, and hide Hypixel clutter.

Smarter Queue Tools — Nick Search can automatically search when the queue fills, and Party Tracker can announce the number of suspected parties.

Quality of Life & Bug Fixes — Highlight text in input boxes, hide Quick Maths titles and output, and receive an alert 10 seconds before a game starts when a challenge is active. Heavy optimisations and fixes across Quick Maths, Nick Search, keybinds, and overlay colours help smooth your playing experience.
